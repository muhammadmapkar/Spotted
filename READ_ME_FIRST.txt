BEFORE POSTING
- Replace every [bracket] placeholder (restaurant name, logo, branches, offer, @handles, city, photos).
- Brand fonts: Poppins ExtraBold (headings) and DM Sans (body text), both free on Google Fonts.
  These exported files use Poppins Bold and Inter instead, which are close matches.
  The designs in the online kit use the exact brand fonts.
- No people, faces, eyes or hands appear in any design.
- The animations are flat, illustrated motion graphics. For realistic footage, use the Google Flow prompts in 00_Plan.
