- **Image (1080×1350):** one still card. The question goes big at the top and a one-line answer sits at the bottom, with one kit visual (phone screen, pin, storefront or stamp card). No paragraphs.
- **Kit reused:** the road, red bookmark banner, phone screens (Saffron Lane, Al Noor Kitchen), map with the 15 km circle, notification, stamp card, creator stars, end titles from your 3 intro videos.
