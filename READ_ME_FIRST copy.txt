BEFORE POSTING
- Replace every [bracket] placeholder (handle, links, place names, prices, etc.).
- Only promise what the app really does (distances, background alerts, Android/iPhone, price).
- Brand fonts are Poppins ExtraBold and DM Sans. These exported files use Poppins Bold and Inter (close matches).
- No people, faces, eyes or hands appear in any design.
