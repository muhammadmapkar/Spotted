# Spotted Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

Spotted helps you remember places you find on Instagram reels and reminds you when you are nearby.
This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

## The short version

- Your saved places and settings stay **on your phone**. We have no servers and no accounts.
- Your location is used **on your phone** to remind you when you are near a saved place. We never receive it.
- We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The places you save: name, address, map position, Google place ID, your category and note, and the reel link you attached.
- Your settings: reminders on/off, distance, and quiet hours.
- A note that you finished the welcome screens.

This information never leaves your phone. You can erase all of it any time: **Settings → Delete all my data**.
Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Location

Spotted asks for your location so it can:
- show how far your saved places are, and put search results near you;
- remind you when you are near a saved place, **even when the app is closed** (this needs the
  "Allow all the time" location permission).

Your location is processed on your phone only. You can turn reminders off in Settings, or change the
location permission in your phone settings. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

## Notifications

Spotted shows notifications on your phone only, to tell you that you are near a saved place.
They are created by the app on your phone. No notification server is involved.

## Information shared with other services

- **Google Places:** when you search for a place, the text you type and your approximate location (to
  prefer nearby results) are sent to Google. Google handles it under its own privacy policy.
  The requests pass through a small relay server that only forwards them to Google (it adds our access key).
  The relay does not store your searches or your location.
- **Captions:** if you paste a caption, or share a TikTok link, the words in the caption that look like a
  place name are sent to Google in the same way, to suggest the place. For a TikTok link, the link is also sent
  to TikTok's public service to get the caption. Nothing is sent unless you do this, and you always confirm the place.
- **Google Maps:** the Map tab is drawn by Google Maps. **Directions** opens Google Maps.
- **Instagram:** Spotted does not connect to Instagram and does not read reels or any Instagram
  content. It only keeps the link you share or paste. **Open reel** opens that link in Instagram or your browser.

## Children

Spotted is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and explain what changed.
