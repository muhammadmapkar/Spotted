# Spotted Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Spotted helps you remember places you find on Instagram or TikTok reels and reminds you when you are nearby.
This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

## The short version

- Your saved places and settings stay **on your phone**. We have no accounts and no database of users.
- Your location is used **on your phone** to remind you when you are near a saved place. We never receive it.
- We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics or tracking.
- You can erase everything any time: **Settings → Delete all my data**.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The places you save: name, address, map position, Google place ID, your category and note, the reel link you attached,
  and which of your collections it is in.
- Your collections (the lists you make, like "Dubai brunch spots").
- Your settings: reminders on/off, distance, daily limit, and quiet hours.
- Small notes the app needs to work: that you finished the welcome screens, and when a reminder was last sent for
  each place (so it does not remind you twice in 24 hours).

This information never leaves your phone. You can erase all of it any time with **Settings → Delete all my data**.
Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, we cannot recover it for you.

## Location

Spotted asks for your location so it can:
- show how far your saved places are, and prefer search results near you;
- remind you when you are near a saved place, **even when the app is closed**. This needs the
  "Allow all the time" (background) location permission.

Your location is processed on your phone only. It is not sent to us or stored by us.
You can turn reminders off in Settings, or change the location permission in your phone's settings at any time.
The rest of the app keeps working without it.

## Notifications

Spotted shows notifications on your phone to tell you that you are near a saved place.
They are created by the app on your phone. No notification server is involved.

## Information shared with other services

- **Google Places (place search):** when you search for a place, the text you type and your approximate location
  (to prefer nearby results) are sent to Google. Google handles it under
  [its own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  The requests pass through a small relay server (hosted on Cloudflare) that only forwards them to Google and adds our
  access key. The relay does not keep a copy of your searches or your location. Cloudflare, like any web host,
  briefly sees the internet address (IP) of the request so it can deliver it and block abuse.
- **Captions:** if you paste a caption, or share a TikTok link, the words in the caption that look like a place name
  are sent to Google in the same way, to suggest the place. For a TikTok link, the link is also sent to TikTok's public
  oEmbed service to get the caption. Nothing is sent unless you do this, and you always confirm the place yourself.
- **Google Maps:** the Map tab on Android is drawn by Google Maps, which may collect technical information such as your
  device and IP address under Google's privacy policy. On iPhone the map is drawn by Apple Maps.
  **Directions** opens Google Maps (or Apple Maps) on your phone.
- **Instagram and TikTok:** Spotted does not connect to your Instagram or TikTok account and does not read reels,
  comments or any content from them. It only keeps the link you share or paste. **Open reel** opens that link in
  Instagram, TikTok or your browser.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

## How long we keep information

Your saved information stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not keep any copy.

## Your choices and rights

- Look at, change or delete any place at any time inside the app.
- Delete everything with **Settings → Delete all my data**.
- Turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings.
- Because we hold no personal information about you on our side, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or erase
  from our systems. If you have any question about your privacy, write to us (see Contact).

## Children

Spotted is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and explain what changed. If we ever add accounts or
cloud storage, we will update this policy first.
