# Message to invite testers

Copy the message below into WhatsApp, Instagram DM or email. Replace the parts in `[square brackets]`.
Send the **day 7** and **day 14** questions later (they are further down).

> **Important for you (Sarah):**
> - Testers need a **Gmail address** (the Google account they use on their Android phone). Collect it first and add it to
>   the tester list in Play Console (see `docs/MY_STEPS.md`, Phase 4G).
> - Google needs at least **12 testers** to stay joined for **14 days**. Invite 15 to 20 people.
> - Testers on **iPhone** cannot join this test yet (iPhone comes later through TestFlight).

## 1. Invitation message

```
Hi [name]! 👋
I made an app called Spotted and I need testers (Android phones). It takes about 5 minutes to set up, then you just use it
normally for 2 weeks.

What it does: you see a reel about a café, restaurant or shop on Instagram or TikTok, you share it to Spotted,
confirm the place, and later when you walk near it your phone reminds you. 📍

HOW TO JOIN (3 steps)
1. Send me the Gmail address you use on your phone. I will add you. (I need it first or the link will not work.)
2. Open this link on your phone and tap "Become a tester": [PASTE THE PLAY CONSOLE JOIN LINK]
3. Then tap "Download it on Google Play" and install Spotted.

WHEN YOU OPEN IT
• Allow location. When it asks, choose "Allow all the time" (this is how it can remind you when the app is closed).
• Allow notifications.
• Your places stay only on your phone. No account needed.

WHAT TO DO (please try for 2 weeks)
• At least 3 times a week: when you see a reel about a place, tap Share → Spotted, and confirm the place.
• Then just live your life. When you get a reminder "You're near …", tap it.
• If a reminder annoys you, open that place and turn off "Remind me when I'm near".
• Please stay joined as a tester for the full 14 days (do not leave the test early). 🙏

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Message me with a screenshot and your phone model. Crashes, reminders that never came, and reminders that came late are
the most useful things to tell me.

I will send you 5 quick questions on day 7 and day 14. Thank you so much!! 💛
```

## 2. Day 7 message

```
Hi [name]! It's been a week with Spotted. 5 quick questions, short answers are perfect:
1. How many places did you save? (You can see it in the My Places list.)
2. How many reminders did you get, and how many were useful?
3. Did you ever go somewhere because of a reminder?
4. Did anything annoy you, or use a lot of battery?
5. Would you be upset if the app disappeared?
Also: did the app ever crash, or did a reminder not arrive or arrive late? Thank you! 💛
```

1. How many places did you save in total?
2. How many reminders did you get, and how many were useful?
3. Did you ever go somewhere because of a reminder? (Which place?)

## 4. What the answers mean

Taken from `docs/PILOT.md`:

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Places saved | Is saving easy enough? |
| Reminders and useful ones | Are reminders worth it? |
| Went somewhere because of a reminder | The key proof the idea works |
| Annoyed / battery | Reliability and fatigue |
| Upset if it disappeared | Real value |

**Keep going** if most testers saved 5 or more places and at least a third acted on a reminder.
**Fix saving** if people save fewer than 3 places. **Fix reminders** if they are late, missing or annoying.
Paste all answers to Claude and ask: "Summarise the pilot results and tell me what to fix first."
